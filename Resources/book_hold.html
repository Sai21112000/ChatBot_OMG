Book & Hold FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Why don't I pay online?⌄

Bhutan Airlines does not sell tickets directly on this website. All bookings from Thailand are handled by OMG Experience, the airline's appointed General Sales Agent (GSA). When you submit a Book & Hold request, no card details are taken and no online payment is accepted. When you are ready to pay, OMG staff will advise you on bank transfer.

What happens after I submit my request?⌄

Within 24 hours, a reservation officer contacts you by email or phone, checks seat availability, confirms the fare and explains the hold period before any payment.

How long are my seats held?⌄

Hold length varies by fare type, travel dates and demand. Your officer confirms the exact expiry when they contact you. Submitting a request does not guarantee seats until availability is confirmed.

Are the fares on this site final?⌄

Fares shown are starting prices in THB inclusive of taxes. The OMG Experience Team provides a written quote for your party before any payment.

Is booking through OMG Experience official?⌄

Yes. OMG Experience is the appointed General Sales Agent (GSA) of Bhutan Airlines in Thailand — the official local sales channel, not a third-party reseller.

What if I need to change or cancel my booking?⌄

Change and cancellation rules depend on the fare type confirmed at booking. Your reservation officer will explain the applicable fees and deadlines before you pay, so there are no surprises later.

Who can I contact for help before I book?⌄

Call +66 2 630 4600 or visit our Travel Information pages for baggage, check-in and visa guidance before you fly.
