Before you fly · Bhutan

Prepare for Paro (PBH)

Check-in, timing and local tips — including via Kolkata.

Documents
Passport valid 6 months. Carry visa approval and ticket — digital and printed.
Check-in · BKK
Zone K · Gates Zone D-1A. Counters open 3 hours before, close 45 minutes before; gates 25 minutes before. Groups 9+: arrive 2 hours early.
When to arrive
BKK departures: 3 hours before. Via Kolkata: allow extra time. Domestic Bhutan: 90 minutes before.
On arrival
Altitude: ~2,200 m — pace day one. Temples: cover shoulders and knees. Currency: BTN (pegged to INR).

Baggage

What's the baggage allowance?

International policy for Bhutan Airlines flights from Bangkok. Allowances may vary by route and cabin — your ticket confirms the final allowance.

Allowance by route & cabin

Checked and carry-on limits for international sectors from Bangkok. Paro, Gaya and Kolkata may differ — your ticket confirms the final allowance.

Route Economy checked Business checked Carry-on
Bangkok ⇄ Paro (PBH) 30 kg 40 kg 7 kg
Bangkok ⇄ Gaya (GAY) 25 kg 30 kg 7 kg
Bangkok ⇄ Kolkata (CCU) 20 kg 30 kg 7 kg

Checked baggage per passenger. Carry-on max 55 × 40 × 20 cm — confirmed at booking and may vary by fare.

Cabin baggage rules

One carry-on piece per passenger. Bags must fit under the seat in front of you or in an overhead locker — not in aisles or near emergency exits.

  • Maximum 7 kg and 55 × 40 × 20 cm
  • Liquids, gels and aerosols follow international security limits
  • Excess carry-on may be checked at the gate

Liquids & personal items

Maximum 100 ml per container, packed in one re-sealable transparent bag (max 1 litre) — one bag per passenger at security. Exemptions apply for baby food, medicines and special dietary needs.

Excess & sports equipment

Contact the OMG Experience Team before travel to pre-purchase extra baggage or arrange approval for sports gear.

Lost or delayed baggage

Report missing baggage at the airport desk before leaving the arrivals hall and obtain a Property Irregularity Report (PIR).

Special assistance

Assistance for every journey

Request when you book — or at least 48 hours before departure.

01
Wheelchair assistance

Available from check-in through to the aircraft door. Up to two mobility aids free of charge in addition to standard baggage.

02
Medical assistance

Contact OMG Experience before booking if you require supplemental oxygen or have a serious medical condition. A fitness-to-fly certificate may be required.

03
Travelling while pregnant

Advise us at booking. Medical clearance may be required for later stages — rules confirmed by the OMG Experience Team.

04
Infants and children

One infant per adult as a lap infant, or in an approved car seat with a separate seat. Child meals and UMNR services may be available — contact us for eligibility.

05
Special meals & accessibility

Vegetarian and other special meals on request. Include requirements in your Book & Hold request or call +66 2 630 4600.

Official General Sales Agent

Your official booking channel

You do not pay online. Every Bhutan Airlines booking from Thailand goes through the OMG Experience Team, the airline's appointed General Sales Agent — never a third-party reseller. Send a request, we confirm seats and fare with you personally, and you pay only when everything is agreed.
