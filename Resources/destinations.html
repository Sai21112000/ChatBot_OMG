Destinations FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What destinations does Bhutan Airlines fly to from Bangkok?⌄

Bhutan Airlines flies from Bangkok Suvarnabhumi (BKK) to Paro, Bhutan (PBH), Gaya (GAY) for Bodh Gaya pilgrimages, and Kolkata, India (CCU). Paro and Kolkata are served regularly; Gaya operates on seasonal dates aligned with pilgrimage seasons.

How do I book a Bhutan Airlines flight from Thailand?⌄

Use the Book & Hold widget on this page or on any destination guide. The OMG Experience Team — Bhutan Airlines' official General Sales Agent in Thailand — confirms availability, fare and travel advice before any payment is taken.

Can I fly Bangkok to Bhutan via Kolkata?⌄

Yes. Many services to Paro (PBH) route via Kolkata (CCU). Kolkata is also ideal if you want to combine an India city break with onward travel to Bhutan on a single itinerary.

What is the cheapest route from Bangkok?⌄

One-way fares to Gaya (GAY) start from THB 11,166 all-inclusive on seasonal services. Kolkata starts from THB 10,325 one way. Return fares to Paro start from THB 17,853. Final fares are confirmed at booking.

Do I need a visa for Paro, Gaya or Kolkata?⌄

Bhutan (Paro): visa and Sustainable Development Fee required for most visitors. India (Gaya & Kolkata): e-Tourist Visa or visa required for most nationalities, including travellers from Thailand. The OMG Experience Team can guide you to official requirements.

Are group or charter flights available?⌄

Yes — especially on the Gaya route. Group return fares from THB 15,000 are available for 10 passengers and above. Custom dates for pilgrimage groups of up to 50–60 can be arranged through the OMG Experience Team.
