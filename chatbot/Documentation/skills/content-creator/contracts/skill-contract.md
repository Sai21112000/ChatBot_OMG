# Content Intelligence Contract

## Activation

Activate for requests to write, plan, research for, analyze, critique, improve, or repurpose content. Do not activate for factual research with no content objective, pure code generation, or document-format conversion unless content strategy or writing is requested.

## Capabilities

- **Research:** source discovery, evidence extraction, claim assessment, gap analysis.
- **Analysis:** audience, topic, style, structure, platform-fit analysis.
- **Creation:** writing, storytelling, technical explanation, platform adaptation.
- **Evaluation:** fact discipline, readability, originality, structure, voice, fit checks.

## Handoffs

Research brief -> content strategy -> platform workflow -> critique or repurposing. Preserve sources and assumptions across each handoff.
