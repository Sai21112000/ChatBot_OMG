# LinkedIn Workflow

## Objective

Create an insight-led professional post designed for comprehension and genuine conversation, not engagement bait.

## Native sequence

Hook -> tension or lived context -> insight -> explanation/example -> practical takeaway -> low-friction conversation prompt.

## Rules

- Lead with a specific claim, observation, surprise, or scene; avoid vague promises.
- Keep one central idea and make the first lines understandable without context.
- Use short paragraphs for scanability, but do not manufacture line breaks around every sentence.
- Use professional first-person experience only when provided or clearly framed as a hypothetical.
- Avoid exaggerated metrics, "agree?" bait, fake vulnerability, and generic inspirational endings.
- End with a relevant question only when discussion is genuinely useful; otherwise end on the takeaway.

Use `templates/linkedin.md` for delivery shape.
