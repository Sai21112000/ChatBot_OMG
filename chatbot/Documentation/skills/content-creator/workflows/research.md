# Research Workflow

## Goal

Create an evidence-aware research brief that can support trustworthy content.

## Process

1. Define the decision or content question; list scope, audience, publication date, and claims needing verification.
2. Search primary sources first: official documentation, original papers, standards, datasets, filings, and direct statements.
3. Add reputable secondary sources only for interpretation, context, or competing viewpoints.
4. Create a claim ledger: claim, source, source type, date, confidence, and caveat.
5. Separate facts from inferences, opinions, examples, and unverified hypotheses.
6. Identify saturation: common framings, repeated examples, stale advice, and unsupported consensus.
7. Identify gaps: unaddressed reader questions, missing implementation detail, overlooked trade-offs, and relevant lived experience.
8. Recommend 3-5 viable angles ranked by relevance, novelty, evidence strength, and author credibility.

## Output

- Research question and scope
- Key findings with source links
- Claim ledger and caveats
- Audience implications
- Saturated narratives and information gaps
- Ranked content angles
- Suggested outline or next action
