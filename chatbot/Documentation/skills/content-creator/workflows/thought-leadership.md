# Thought Leadership Workflow

Make a defensible point of view rather than restating industry consensus.

Ground the thesis in evidence, direct experience supplied by the author, or a transparent interpretation. Steelman the alternative view, state boundary conditions, and show consequences for the reader. Do not manufacture certainty or controversy.

Recommended forms: assumption -> tension -> reframing -> evidence/example -> practical implication; or common advice -> why it fails -> better model -> application.
